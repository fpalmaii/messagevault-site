Your vault stays on your phone

Search never leaves your device. When you type in the search box, nothing is sent anywhere. The search runs against a database on your phone — which is why it works with no signal, returns instantly, and costs nothing to run.

We don't keep your library. Your saved posts live on your phone, not on our servers. To generate tags, a short text snippet and a small preview thumbnail are sent to be described — both taken from the public post you saved. Nothing else leaves. Not the message you got it from, not who sent it, not your search history.

There's nothing to sell. MessageVault costs $1.99 a month, and that's the entire business model. No ads, no affiliate links, no analytics on what you save, no data brokers. When an app is free, something else is paying for it.
